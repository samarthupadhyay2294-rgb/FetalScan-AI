# User Guide

## Uploading an Image

1. Navigate to **Upload** from the navbar
2. Optionally enter a Patient ID
3. Drag & drop or click to select a fetal HC ultrasound image
4. Click **Run AI Prediction**

## Reading Results

The prediction dashboard shows:

- **Original & Annotated images** with landmarks A, B, C, D
- **BPD / OFD** in millimeters
- **Cephalic Index** percentage
- **Confidence** gauge from heatmap peaks
- **Disease screening** with risk percentage
- **Interactive charts** for risk and measurements

## Downloading Reports

Click **Download PDF** to save a clinical-style report with QR code and signature area.

## Viewing History

The **Dashboard** page lists all previous predictions stored in SQLite.

## Important

This tool is for **research and educational screening only**. Always consult qualified medical professionals for clinical decisions.
